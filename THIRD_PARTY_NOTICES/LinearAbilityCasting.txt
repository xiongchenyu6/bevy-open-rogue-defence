Ported and adapted for this Bevy game:
- src/assets/ProceduralGeometry.js crystal ring and bent-axis geometry
- src/materials/LightningMaterial.js octave linear-noise bolt filaments
- src/materials/BeamMaterial.js helical coil geometry and core/sheath treatment
- src/effects/GroundFissures.js tapered random-walk ground cracks and branches
- src/shaders/lib/noise.glsl.js procedural noise
- src/materials/FrostFieldMaterial.js boundary/finger/ridged flow treatment
- src/abilities/GlacierAbility.js springy emergence

The Rust/WGSL implementations use shared bounded game resources and authoritative
skill positions, ranges and timers. No third-party character or HDR assets from
the reference repository are included.
