Camera transforms, lens and screenshot image targets retain the production view. No product-only harness hook is added.
The safe choice's gold delta is at least the promised small_gold because legitimate passive income can accrue on the same frame. Its wood delta is exactly small_wood, fruit remains unchanged, and no relic is owned. It creates no boss or guards. A duplicate TakeReward event on the native input-message channel during a paused hold leaves the entire wallet unchanged, proving it cannot pay again.
All three camp fights rent their actual boss and three guards. Real primary warnings, hazard damage, half-health phase and secondary hazards are observed. Warning timers and the remaining battle timer freeze during pause. Fixture HP thresholds expose phase and speed up finishing; actual ordinary attacks and the authoritative damage resolver defeat the boss and its guards before the victory reward is accepted.
To isolate exposed-hero combat, the fixture holds the gate down with a long revival prerequisite and gives the hero high finite health; these values are not normal gameplay balance or the normal four-second gate-rebuild duration.
Wood and fruit reward deltas are exact, gold includes any legitimate passive income while fighting. The wallet then remains unchanged during a paused hold, with owned hazards and guards removed. The loss fixture sets one remaining life and low hero HP, lets genuine enemy combat deliver fatal damage and uses the actual retry control to verify a fresh run. During hazard HP probes the real guards remain active; native HP loss does not attribute each point exclusively to a hazard, and authoritative hazard unit tests establish individual damage mechanisms.
Each victory's unique BasicRelics ownership comes from the real award. Four durable ordinary enemies then receive actual ordinary attacks until RelicCast and casts_emitted appear. Ordinary firing stops before checking the named relic spectacle and subsequent off-axis target HP loss. Ice also changes actual Enemy.speed to zero. The safe choice grants none of these relics.
