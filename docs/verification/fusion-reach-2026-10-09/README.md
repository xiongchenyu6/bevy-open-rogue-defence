# Inherited weapon reach

A melee host previously selected only targets inside its own weapon reach. When a target was five metres away, the native attack loop returned before advancing fusion, so an absorbed bow or staff could never attack.

The native weapon retains its original reach. When a native target is absent but an absorbed ranged weapon can reach a living target, actual completed attack-clock slots advance the same four-opportunity fusion cadence. They emit the existing FusionRequest path; they do not create native swings, increment native attack/volley counters, or trigger market/relic native attack effects. Collected heroes do not multiply the opportunity rate.

Eligible deferred slots have one HeroFusion queue. Native volleys and deferred legacy slots share the existing eight-opportunity frame budget. Native target reentry adds no fallback slots, but previously earned eligible slots drain when budget remains. Out-of-range targets, death and finish clear pending legacy slots; pause preserves them. Ranged requests validate actual target reach again at emission.

The executable baseline compiled and failed four production regressions with zero legacy emission where nonzero emission was required. Two other new native/no-target cases and three existing inherited tests passed. Final production full suite R2: 635 passed, zero failed, one ignored. Eight new fixtures exercise real auto_attack and FusionPlugin emission: bow/staff beyond melee reach plus real resolved contact damage, normal versus 32/60-second clock cadence, no out-of-range banking, unchanged native cadence, large-step deferred drainage, no-target queue cleanup, death/finish/pause lifecycle, and full native backlog consuming the shared work budget without duplicating or dropping earlier fallback opportunities.

The native hero-fusion-reach capture intentionally injects two fixture legacies; it is not an earned campaign run or recruitment proof. A level-one StoneWarden stands five metres from a durable stationary target, with skills zero, ultimate disabled and the natural two-attacks-per-second clock. It verifies both legacy emission counters, real damaged target health, observed actual legacy projectile carriers, and zero native attacks. The final two 400-frame runs at 1280×720 and 667×375 passed with bow=3, staff=3, targetHP=99859.61/100000, native attacks=0 and no ultimate visual entities. All twelve final PNGs were personally inspected: blue staff and amber bow carriers travel across the gate, and real contact damage appears beyond the melee host reach. A capture-only after-Update schedule disables selection for PostUpdate ability synchronization/casts; First restores selection before Update combat/UI. This gate is solely fixture isolation. The first capture was rejected because independent inherited ultimate cooldowns were still active despite extending the current hero’s ActiveSkill cooldown; its blue beam/red fan and extra damage are retained as rejected evidence. Final production all-target Clippy/main+capture build passed R4; final isolated capture formatting, strict Clippy and capture build passed R6. Library sources did not change after the 635-test R2 run.

This fix does not prove all arbitrary attack speeds, projectile trajectories or damage are frame invariant. Legacy projectile catch-up travel is unchanged. It also does not prove that this was the cause of a particular campaign loss or that the campaign is now balanced.
