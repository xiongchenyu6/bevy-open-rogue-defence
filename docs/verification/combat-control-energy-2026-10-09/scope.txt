  This test does not claim released hazard damage coverage.
- Active authored clip pause state, unchanged facing/reaction, stale cue suppression and retirement.
  App does not schedule AnimationPlugin playback: seek-time assertion proves no guard playhead mutation,
  not elapsed animation playback equivalence.
- Predicate includes stun and freeze, excludes slow-only and stale zero-duration controls.

Limits:

for partial-frame control expiry; large simulation steps can cross expiry before combat runs.
No all-speed / 32x status or full AI temporal equivalence claim.
No native screenshot alone proves attack suppression; screenshots verify visual regressions only.
