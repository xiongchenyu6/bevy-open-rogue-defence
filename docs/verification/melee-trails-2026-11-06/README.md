# Melee afterimages fade instead of remaining solid cards

This change is confined to six melee weapon trails. Broad opaque orange/purple crescents visibly hid targets and cast hard black shadows in the [actual axe](baseline-axe/frame00014.png)/[Void](baseline-void/frame00012.png) baseline. They retained full alpha until despawn; only two starter swords had a fade. Existing melee outlines, attack timing/range/damage/target budget, layers, colors and tier emission are unchanged. No extra lights or per-hit materials are created. Ranged/projectile materials remain opaque and unchanged.

One startup-only cache replaces the obsolete starter-sword cache: six melee heroes×four growth tiers×six lifetime phases=144 immutable Blend handles. Total isolated attack-art material assets are193, including49 unchanged base/arrow materials. Each trail swaps handles as its own lifetime progresses (.68,.50,.34,.20,.09,.02) and has explicit NotShadowCaster. An older slash cannot be brightened by a newer one; no shared material is mutated. Pause freezes lifetime; finish/expiry removes all layers. Tests exercise real spawn→animate→cleanup for every24class/tier combination, plus repeated swings, instance isolation, pause/finish and fixed asset count. No obsolete starter-only compatibility cache remains.
