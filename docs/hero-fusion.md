# Hero absorption and inheritance

Recruiting replaces the controlled hero and permanently inherits the old hero’s weapons, learned skills, passives and automatic ultimate for the current run.

- Every four successful basic attacks trigger one inherited weapon at 45% of current attack damage. Multiple inherited heroes rotate in order. Echoes cannot trigger themselves. Melee weapons project a spectral strike at the target, so ranged heroes can use inherited swords and axes.
- Learned skill ranks and ascension tiers remain intact. The new hero’s starting skill joins the build without reducing an existing rank. Each newly recruited hero expands the six starting skill slots by one. Returning to an already recruited hero gives neither another slot nor a different free starting skill.
- Inherited passives keep their accumulated progress and remain active together.
- Each ultimate retains its cooldown. Ultimates fire automatically when useful, with at least three seconds between casts and no interruption of a running sequence.
- Recruitment previews inheritance; the hero portrait opens attributes and the inherited arsenal, including weapon, passive and ultimate names.

The inheritance balance and interface are original adaptations for this game.
