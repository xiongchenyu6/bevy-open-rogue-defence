# UR specialisation and skill spectacle

A UR skill reaching rank five offers three exclusive paths at the next skill draw. Choose one path for the run. It keeps rank five, does not enter the old linear ascension ladder, and is retained when the hero is absorbed. Replacing the skill clears its path. The hero attribute sheet displays the selected path.

| UR skill | Offensive path | Control / spell path | Sustain / army path |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frost wyrm | Every 3s, frost surge across up to six enemies | Every 2s, priority-target frost lock | Up to two long-lived wyrms |
| Ancestor colossus | Every 3s, 5.5m slam | Every 1s, 6m gravity pull and brief lock | Every 4s in combat, heal hero/gate by 2% |
| Triune engine | Every fourth basic attack, 70% physical echo | Every 2.5s, spell chain across up to four enemies | Every 3s, up to eight summons strike |
| Fate cube | Every 6s, two hunting beasts, four active maximum | Every 5s, six-target blast and 1s stun | Every 8s in combat, +20 gold/+4 wood |

Routes use authoritative damage, control, summon, healing and economic systems. They pause with battle time and cannot generate unbounded summons or idle currency. The route rules and balance are adaptations for this game.

Skill visuals retain their identities: water waves, lightning chains/grids, fire rain, ice formations, vortex ribbons, viral tendrils, sword formations, saw blades and summon arrivals. Dedicated water fronts, orbit weapons and continuous flame rays keep their physical silhouettes. UR visuals follow real branch action events. Cosmetic particles share a 600-entity budget and do not deal damage.
