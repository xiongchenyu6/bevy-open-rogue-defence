# Equipment inventory

The equipment icon next to the collection and bond controls shows the number of owned Black Market equipment items. Click it or press I to inspect equipment. The panel pauses combat; Escape, I or the close button resumes the previous state.

Every owned item has an icon, a rarity border and a preview of its name and current effect. Clicking a card selects it; desktop hover also previews it. The list scrolls with a mouse wheel or touch drag. An empty inventory explains where to buy equipment. Purchased consumables do not occupy the equipment inventory. Equipment applies automatically and remains active through hero absorption; there is no invented slot limit or unequip operation.

Actual purchases update the count, highlight the inventory entry and show a short acquisition message after the market closes. Run transitions clear the inventory UI and use the new run's real ownership state.

## Crafting

The Recipes tab previews six recipes, marks missing ingredients and shows their inherited effects and additional bonuses. Details scroll independently of the fixed craft button. Crafting consumes active ingredients, retains all their effects and applies the product's additional bonus exactly once. Products appear in the equipment inventory. Acquired ingredients cannot be purchased again after consumption, and a recipe cannot be crafted twice in one expedition. Crafting plays the confirmation sound and highlights the resulting inventory.

| Ingredients | Product | Additional bonus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Serrated Disc + Blood Contract | Bloodreaver | 3 physical armor penetration, 5% lifesteal |
| Arc Lens + Echo Core | Astral Prism | 10 percentage points magic penetration, 15% spell power |
| Hardened Plate + Wind Boots | Storm Bastion | 4 armor, 5% evasion |
| Ember Heart + Storm Coil | Suncoil | 5 percentage points critical chance, 15% spell power |
| Bloodreaver + Storm Bastion + Ascendant Crown | Night Crown | 4 physical armor penetration, 5% lifesteal, 4 armor |
| Astral Prism + Suncoil + Prismatic Engine | World Engine | 10 percentage points magic penetration, 10 percentage points skill echo chance |

Inventory and crafting reset between expeditions. Both languages and desktop/touch layouts use the same ownership and effect calculations.
