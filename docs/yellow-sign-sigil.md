# Yellow Sign elite field

The thick yellow circle is a persistent elite curse field, not an attack warning. Inside its 4.5-unit radius, commander cooldown recovery is halved. Previously the shared field stroke scaled to about 0.5 world units wide, producing a broad flat neon band.

Yellow Sign now has a dedicated shared sigil mesh and translucent amber material. Its continuous outer boundary remains at the same radius, with eight inward ticks and four small diamond marks. The center stays open. Stroke width at gameplay scale is approximately 0.09 units. The bright owner crest, other elite fields, red raid warning, lifetime, pooling, pause behavior and gameplay radius are unchanged.

A geometry regression checks finite vertices, the outer edge at the actual gameplay radius, and an empty actor center. Native validation is queued through the real elite debug-spawn/input flow: enter field, assert 0.5 cooldown recovery, leave field and assert normal recovery. Full library tests passed 596 cases, with 0 failures and 1 existing ignored test. Strict all-target Clippy and both binaries built successfully. Six NVIDIA Vulkan cases completed 6,190 frames: English desktop/compact/portrait and Chinese compact each ran 650 frames; desktop and compact late-wave scenarios each ran 1,795 frames. Real keyboard/debug-spawn and gameplay checks confirmed 0.5 recovery inside the aura and 1.0 after leaving. Both late-wave arrival checks passed.

Root inspected all 14 new screenshots against the old desktop examples. The field boundary and inward marks remain visible in audited views, while the wide neon band is gone and actor centers remain open. Dense actors can naturally occlude sections of the ground sigil; narrow portrait views can show only part of an offscreen field. This is not proof of all hardware/DPI visibility, human comprehension, long-session performance or Steam acceptance.
