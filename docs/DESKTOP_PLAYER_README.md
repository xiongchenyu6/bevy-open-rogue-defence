Rogue Commander

Launch rogue_commander. Keep the assets and third-party folders beside it.
This Linux build targets Steam Linux Runtime 3 (sniper). It needs a compatible
Vulkan graphics driver. It does not require Rust, Nix or a development checkout.

Choose a chapter and a basic commander. Attacks and ultimate abilities fire
automatically. Build your skills and bonds to withstand increasingly strong waves.
The gate has limited health and rebuilds after falling. Your hero has three lives;
losing the last life ends the expedition. Cleared chapters and permanent training
carry over to later expeditions.

Move: W / A / S / D or arrow keys. Dodge: Shift.
Use the skill, equipment, bond and challenge buttons to inspect and grow your build.
Settings offers English or Simplified Chinese, detailed or simple card descriptions,
music and effect volume, and fullscreen. F11 switches fullscreen; Escape closes
an open panel or pauses play. Return to chapters or quit through Settings.
Leaving an unfinished expedition discards its current build and unspent resources.

Preferences and campaign progress are saved in your user configuration directory,
under rogue-commander. They are separate from the installation folder. Keep the
whole package together when moving it. Third-party credits and license texts are
included in third-party and assets/licenses; the UI font's license is assets/fonts/OFL.txt.

This is a development release candidate. Full campaign balance and all supported
hardware combinations are still being tested.
