# Vortex readability revision

The thick opaque spiral is replaced by three thin, tapered wind ribbons with short breaks and peripheral traveling wisps. One shared390-vertex ribbon mesh and dedicated material serve the existing particle budget. Width and horizontal size still follow the actual skill radius. Gameplay damage, range, pull, cooldown and charge are unchanged.

The first revision was too faint. The brighter revision still showed a large purple footprint because generic impact tinting overwrote the custom material, and the separate ground-projection layer retained Arcane-purple additive discs. Vortex now explicitly retains its authored material and uses a low-alpha cool projection. Other spell materials and ground profiles retain their existing rendering. The final native harness asserts the actual loaded ground material alpha0.08, real charged cast telemetry and isolated skill gameplay.
